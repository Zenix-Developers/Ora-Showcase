# Ora

### The assistant that lends your PC a helping hand.

*A desktop AI companion for Windows that opens, browses, writes and organizes alongside you — always with your say-so.*

</div>

---

> **This is a showcase repository.** It contains no source code — only a tour of what Ora is and the experience it creates.

## The idea

Imagine a capable, friendly assistant sitting beside you at your computer. Tell it what you'd like in everyday words, and it gets to work — opening apps, browsing the web, drafting files and handling the little chores that eat up your day.

Ora is built around one gentle promise: **you stay in charge.** It asks before it acts on anything that matters, and the moment you reach for your mouse or keyboard, it steps aside.

## What Ora brings to your day

- **Just say it.** Type a request in plain language and watch it come together.
- **Bots with a purpose.** Give a bot a clear mission — the goal, when it's done, where it may go, and when it should check in with you — and let it carry on while you get on with your life.
- **Teams of helpers.** Ora can bring in sub-agents to share a bigger job, and **Ora Build** lends a hand with coding projects in a tidy workspace of its own.
- **It remembers how you like things.** Once it has done a routine task well, it learns the pattern, so the next time is even smoother.
- **Presentations in a flash.** Turn a brief into an editable deck with polished previews, ready to share as PDF or PPTX.
- **Calm, clear approvals.** Choose how adventurous Ora should be — careful, balanced or bold — with sensible limits that always keep you in control.
